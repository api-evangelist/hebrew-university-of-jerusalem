13 October, 2026 Landmark gift by the Lavin Family, through its foundation, The Sheba Foundation, will launch the Hebrew University of Jerusalem Center for Advanced Medical Education, a new model designed to train more physicians without compromising excellence, preserve the human foundations of medicine, and prepare doctors to lead in an AI-enabled world.
