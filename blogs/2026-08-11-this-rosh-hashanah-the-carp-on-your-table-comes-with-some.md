11 August, 2026 New study finds that while common carp are highly vulnerable to a devastating virus, their close relatives help stop its spread—offering hope for fish farmers and freshwater ecosystems. [Hebrew University of Jerusalem] – For many Israeli families, no Rosh Hashanah table is complete without carp. Whether served as traditional gefilte fish or another festive dish, the fish has long symbolized abundance and prosperity for the year ahead.
