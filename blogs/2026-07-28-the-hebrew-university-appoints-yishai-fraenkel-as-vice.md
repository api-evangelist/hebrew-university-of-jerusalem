28 July, 2026 The Hebrew University of Jerusalem has appointed former Intel Vice President and former Hebrew University Vice President and Director General Yishai Fraenkel as its new Vice President for Advancement and External Relations. Fraenkel will lead the University's global engagement with donors, alumni, friends, and strategic partners as it embarks on its second century. Staff
