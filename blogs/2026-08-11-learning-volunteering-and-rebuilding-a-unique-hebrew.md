11 August, 2026 Students from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem began a unique summer course that combines academic study with agricultural volunteering at Kibbutz Sufa in the Gaza border region. The course integrates academic learning, agricultural volunteering and community activities and is being held in cooperation with Ein Prat – The Academy for Leadership.
